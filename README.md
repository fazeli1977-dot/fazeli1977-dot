# Hi, I'm Fazeli — Geospatial & Environmental Software Engineer

I build reproducible **Earth Observation pipelines**, **hydrological & subsidence analytics**, and **WebGIS decision-support tools** using Python, Google Earth Engine, InSAR/SAR workflows, and modern geospatial stacks.

---

### Core Focus
- **Remote Sensing & EO:** Sentinel-1 (InSAR/SAR), Sentinel-2, Landsat, NDWI/NDVI time-series, Google Earth Engine
- **Water & Environmental Systems:** Lake/wetland monitoring, drought & climate indicators, hydraulic network optimization
- **Geospatial Engineering:** Python (`rasterio`, `geopandas`, `xarray`), PostGIS, FastAPI, interactive dashboards & automated reporting

---

### Featured Projects

- **[Urmia-Lake-Observatory](https://github.com/fazeli1977-dot/Urmia-Lake-Observatory)** — Multi-sensor lake surface & hydrological monitoring pipeline with explicit separation between observed satellite data and modeled/simulated outputs.
- **[Tehran-Subsidence-Observatory](https://github.com/fazeli1977-dot/Tehran-Subsidence-Observatory)** — InSAR-based ground deformation analysis and spatial risk visualization for urban subsidence monitoring.
- **[SAR-MotionLens](https://github.com/fazeli1977-dot/SAR-MotionLens)** — SAR/InSAR motion analysis toolkit for surface displacement screening.
- **[water-network-optimizer](https://github.com/fazeli1977-dot/water-network-optimizer)** — Computational tools for water distribution network analysis and hydraulic optimization.
