## Featured Projects

### Urmia Lake Observatory — Open Geospatial Monitoring

[Open the live observatory](https://urmia-lake-observatory.pages.dev/) · [View the repository](https://github.com/fazeli1977-dot/Urmia-Lake-Observatory)

A bilingual Persian/English geospatial observatory for Lake Urmia, combining satellite-altimetry records, climate reanalysis, GIS layers, and interactive time-series and map views. Data products distinguish direct observations from derived or modeled indicators, with unavailable measurements identified explicitly.

### SAR-MotionLens — Multi-Frequency SAR/InSAR Lab

[Open the live application](https://sar-motionlens.streamlit.app/) · [View the repository](https://github.com/fazeli1977-dot/SAR-MotionLens)

Explore X-, C- (Sentinel-1), and L-band SAR workflows, including interferograms, time series, anomaly scoring, and downloadable reports. The app queries real NISAR granule metadata from ASF DAAC.

> Transparency: deformation, interferogram, time-series, and machine-learning outputs are physically motivated simulations, not satellite-derived measurements. Only the NISAR granule metadata is queried live from ASF DAAC.

### Water Network Optimizer

[View the repository](https://github.com/fazeli1977-dot/water-network-optimizer)

Pressurized pipe sizing with velocity checks and Hazen–Williams head-loss calculations, with CSV export and a command-line interface.

### FireSync MODIS–VIIRS

[View the repository](https://github.com/fazeli1977-dot/FireSync-MODIS-VIIRS)

A multi-sensor wildfire monitoring workflow that harmonizes MODIS and VIIRS hotspot observations. The dashboard uses deterministic synthetic examples; live FIRMS ingestion has not been demonstrated.

### Water Sampling App

[View the repository](https://github.com/fazeli1977-dot/water-sampling-app)
