## Featured Projects


### Tehran Subsidence Observatory — Real Sentinel-1A/1D SBAS & 2D InSAR

[Open the live observatory](https://cd1fb290.tehran-subsidence-observatory.pages.dev/) · [View the repository](https://github.com/fazeli1977-dot/Tehran-Subsidence-Observatory)

End-to-end InSAR processing and bilingual WebGIS monitoring land subsidence across all **16 counties of Tehran Province** using real **Sentinel-1A/1D IW TOPS SLC** acquisitions (2026-04-03 to 2026-11-05), AUX_POEORB orbits, SNAPHU unwrapping, curvature-aware IRLS detrending, and Ascending+Descending 2D decomposition (-364 mm/yr peak in SW Tehran Plain) with zero extrapolation outside acquired bursts.
### Urmia Lake Observatory — Open Geospatial Monitoring

[Open the live observatory](https://urmia-lake-observatory.pages.dev/) · [View the repository](https://github.com/fazeli1977-dot/Urmia-Lake-Observatory) · [![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.24554247.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24554247)

A bilingual Persian/English geospatial observatory for Lake Urmia, combining satellite-altimetry records, climate reanalysis, GIS layers, and interactive time-series and map views. Data products distinguish direct observations from derived or modeled indicators, with unavailable measurements identified explicitly.

[![Urmia Lake Observatory executive dashboard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fazeli1977-dot/Urmia-Lake-Observatory/main/docs/screenshots/01-overview-kpis.png)](https://urmia-lake-observatory.pages.dev/)

[Browse all five project screenshots](https://github.com/fazeli1977-dot/Urmia-Lake-Observatory/tree/main/docs/screenshots)

### SAR-MotionLens — Multi-Frequency SAR/InSAR Lab

[Open the live application](https://sar-motionlens.streamlit.app/) · [View the repository](https://github.com/fazeli1977-dot/SAR-MotionLens)

Interactive X-, C- (Sentinel-1), and L-band SAR workflows including interferograms, time series, anomaly scoring, and downloadable reports, with live NISAR granule metadata queried from ASF DAAC.

> **Transparency:** Deformation, interferogram, time-series, and machine-learning outputs are physically motivated simulations, not satellite-derived measurements. Only NISAR granule metadata is queried live from ASF DAAC.

### Water Network Optimizer

[View the repository](https://github.com/fazeli1977-dot/water-network-optimizer) · [![CI](https://github.com/fazeli1977-dot/water-network-optimizer/actions/workflows/ci.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/fazeli1977-dot/water-network-optimizer/actions/workflows/ci.yml)

A Python toolkit and CLI for pressurized pipe sizing, velocity checks, and Hazen–Williams head-loss calculations, with CSV export and automated `pytest` coverage.

### Water Sampling App

[View the repository](https://github.com/fazeli1977-dot/water-sampling-app)

A Persian RTL workflow for water and wastewater sampling, geotagged records, lab-result comparisons against Iranian DOE standards, and PDF reporting. Compliance checks are indicative and do not replace official laboratory or regulatory determinations.
